NetShare Privacy Policy

App: NetShare (au.event.netshare) · Developer: sgateori · Last updated: 2026-10-22

NetShare runs the open-source copyparty file server on your phone and lets you send files to other people with a link. The files are served from your phone. There is no NetShare server, no NetShare account and no ads. The developer does not receive, store or see the files you share. This page says what stays on your phone, what leaves it, and who can see it.

What stays on your device

Your shared files, the staged copies you have not yet published, your owner username and password, your link list and each link's settings (recipient label, download limit, expiry), the per-link activity log (time, the recipient's IP address, and whether it was a download, a resume or a blocked attempt; up to 500 rows per link), any Cloudflare tunnel token you enter, and the app's logs are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. The app does not upload any of this to the developer. Android cloud backup is turned off for the app.

The app does not ask for storage permissions. Files reach it through Android's share sheet or the system document picker, and are copied into the app's own folder. A staged copy is only published when you tap Create link. Cancelling before that removes the staged copy, and revoking a link ends access to it. Uninstalling the app deletes its data.

What recipients and others can see

Anyone who has a link can open it and download what you shared with it, until it expires, reaches its download limit or you revoke it. Anyone with your owner login can see everything in your shared folder. The app has no content moderation. Send links only to people you trust.

How shared files travel

You choose how to share. In each case the traffic goes between your phone and your recipients, not through the developer.

  • Quick tunnel (default): the app asks Cloudflare (api.trycloudflare.com) for a public HTTPS address, then a bundled cloudflared program connects your phone out to Cloudflare's network. Cloudflare therefore handles the traffic, including the content, link paths, your phone's IP address and your recipients' IP addresses, and can technically see them because it ends the HTTPS connection at its edge. Cloudflare's own policy applies: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Named tunnel (optional): the same path, using your own Cloudflare account, domain and tunnel token. Cloudflare is then your own provider.
  • LAN only: no tunnel and no third party. Your phone answers on its Wi-Fi address using plain http inside your local network, so other devices on that network could read the traffic.

Security scan

The Scan tab checks your setup. It runs automatically the first time sharing starts and again after some other actions, and when you tap Scan. This does not depend on your crash-report choice below. It sends the names and versions of the libraries bundled in the app to the OSV vulnerability database (api.osv.dev) to look up known issues. No personal data and no files are sent, but OSV sees your phone's IP address like any web server would. It also requests your own public share address (user agent NetShare-selfprobe) to test whether your server can be reached from outside. Shared files are also checked for secrets, on the phone only.

Crash reports and anonymous usage (Firebase)

This is off by default. On first launch the app asks "Help improve NetShare?" and you must choose Allow or No thanks. Nothing is sent until you choose Allow. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, Privacy. If you decline or switch it off, queued crash reports are deleted. Collection is also always off in debug builds.

If you allow it, the app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. Google acts as a service provider processing this data for us.

The app does not send share links, tunnel addresses, recipient names, file names, passwords, the Cloudflare tunnel token or IP addresses in these reports, and it does not use the advertising ID.

Limit: if the app crashes with an uncaught error, the error text written by the system or a library is sent as it is. The app removes the message from the handled problems it reports itself, but not from uncaught crashes. In rare cases such text, for example from the embedded Python runtime, might include a file path or the name of a shared file. This only applies after you have allowed collection.

Google may see your phone's IP address when the upload happens and may derive an approximate country or region from it. The app itself never reads your location.

Data is sent over HTTPS. Retention is set by Google's defaults for Firebase. See Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your files, file names, links, recipient labels, passwords, tunnel token, contacts, photos, location from your device, advertising ID, or any account or personal information. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

Permissions

Internet (tunnel, Firebase, security scan); network and Wi-Fi state (your LAN address and connection type); notifications (the sharing notification); a foreground service of the special-use type with a wake lock (keeps your files reachable while you share, even with the screen off); and a permission to open Android's screen for ignoring battery optimisation, used only when you tap the button for it in Settings. No storage, location, contacts, camera or microphone access. None of these send data to the developer.

Your choices and deleting data

Turn crash reports and usage statistics off at any time in Settings, Privacy. Revoke a link or change its expiry. Stop the server to stop serving. Choose LAN only to avoid third parties. Delete data on your phone in the app or by uninstalling. We have no account system and no file data, so we cannot look up your data by name. To ask us to delete reports tied to your Firebase installation ID, email us that ID or a description of when a crash happened, and we will delete what we can match.

Children

NetShare is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children.
